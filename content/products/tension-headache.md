## Short Description of Tension Headache

Tension headaches refer to generally a mild to moderate pain described as feeling like a tight band around the head.It is the most common type of headache but the causes are not fully known.

## Symptoms of Tension Headache

- Dull, aching head pain
- Sensation of pressure or tightness across the forehead or on the sides and back of the head
- Tenderness in the scalp, neck and shoulder muscles

## About Tension Headache

Headaches in general are a common health issue. Almost everybody has experienced it at some point in their life.However, not all headaches are the same.As a matter of fact there are more than a hundred types according to medical experts.For most, headaches lead to minor aches or pains that happen on occasion.Less commonly, more severe headaches like migraines result to throbbing pain and may make routing tasks difficult if not impossible to do.Rarely, severe sudden head pain can be a sign of a serious health issue like a brain tumor.

Tension headaches are the most common kind of headache.They cause mild-to-moderate pain around the head, face or neck.They do not cause other symptoms like nausea or vomiting.Doctors do not really consider tension headaches to be dangerous.

## Etiology of Tension Headaches

There is no single cause for tension headaches according to researchers that have already been identified.This kind of headache is not hereditary. Many healthcare providers believe it is the result of tightened muscles in the back of the head or neck that trigger it.Most people do not always seek a consult from a doctor for a simple tension headache and this makes it hard to predict exact numbers.Research estimates that as much as 2 in 3 adults in the US get tension headaches.Chronic tension headaches are much less common.They affect an estimated 3% of adults.Women more than men suffer from tension headaches.
