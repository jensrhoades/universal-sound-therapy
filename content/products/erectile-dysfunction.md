## Short Description of Erectile Dysfunction

Erectile dysfunction or ED is the most common sexual problem that men report to their healthcare provider. It is the condition where men find difficulty getting or keeping an erection during sex.

## Symptoms of Erectile Dysfunction

Erectile dysfunction symptoms might include persistent:

- Trouble getting an erection
- Trouble keeping an erection
- Reduced sexual desire

## About Erectile Dysfunction

Erectile dysfunction (ED) affects as much as 30 million men. ED is the inability to get or keep an erection firm enough for sexual intercourse. Issues when getting or keeping an erection may also be a sign of an underlying medical problem that requires treatment. Erectile dysfunction is also risk factor for heart disease.

## Etiology of Erectile Dysfunction

The male sexual arousal process is complex. It involves the brain, hormones, emotions, nerves, muscles and blood vessels. ED may occur as a result of a problem from any of these.

Physical causes of erectile dysfunction include:

- Heart disease
- Clogged blood vessels (atherosclerosis)
- High cholesterol
- High blood pressure
- Diabetes
- Obesity
- Metabolic syndrome
- Parkinson's disease
- Multiple sclerosis
- Certain prescription medications
- Tobacco use
- Peyronie's disease
- Alcoholism and other forms of substance abuse
- Sleep disorders
- Treatments for prostate cancer or enlarged prostate
- Surgeries or injuries that affect the pelvic area or spinal cord
- Low testosterone

Psychological causes include:

- Depression, anxiety or other mental health conditions
- Stress at home, work or worry about sexual performance
- Stress from social, cultural or religious conflicts
- Relationship problems

## Risk Factors

- Medical conditions, particularly diabetes or heart conditions
- Tobacco use
- Overweight and obese patients
- Certain medical treatments
- Injuries, particularly if they damage the nerves or arteries that control erections
- Medications, including antidepressants, antihistamines and medications to treat high blood pressure
- Psychological conditions, such as stress, anxiety or depression
- Drug and alcohol use

## Diagnosis of Erectile Dysfunction

Testing for erectile dysfunction involves a series of steps including physical examination, taking the health and sexual history of the patient, and additional tests such as ultrasound, nocturnal penile tumescence test, injection test, urine test, and blood tests.
