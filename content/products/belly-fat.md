## Short Description of Belly Fat

Belly fat is basically body fat that has accumulated in the abdomen or mid-section of the body. It results from poor diet, aging, genetics, and lack of exercise and alcohol intake.

## About Belly Fat

Belly fat is associated with a number of health risks. The trouble with this is not limited to the additional layer of padding located just below the skin (subcutaneous fat) but also includes the visceral fat, which is located deep within the abdomen as well as the surrounding organs.

Regardless of your overall weight, patients with an expanding abdominal girth are at increased risk of developing: Heart Disease, Type 2 Diabetes, Colon Cancer, Sleep Apnea, Premature death from any cause, Hypertension.

### Role of Sugary Foods and Beverages

A high sugar diet contributes to excess abdominal fat. Sugar-sweetened beverages make it quite easy to drink large amounts of sugar in a very short period of time resulting to excess abdominal fat.

### Alcohol

Heavy alcohol consumption is clearly linked to excess abdominal fat — hence the term "beer belly."

### Trans Fats

A diet rich in trans fats is linked to higher abdominal fat.

### Sedentary Lifestyle

An inactive lifestyle is considered one of the biggest factors to poor health and has contributed to higher rates of obesity including excess abdominal fat.
