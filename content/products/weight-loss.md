## Short Description of Overweight

According to the National Institutes of Health, being overweight is defined as having a body mass index of 27.3% or more for women and 27.8% or more for men.

### Symptoms of Being Overweight

- A noticeable weight gain that if measured by body mass index is greater than 27.8% for men and 27.3% for women
- You often have to buy new clothing all the time because your clothes no longer fit you
- You snore more loudly than before
- Presence of tender spots all over your body
- Frequently tired
- Always hungry all the time
- Presence of high blood pressure and high cholesterol levels
- Waist circumference of greater than 35 inches

### Becoming Overweight

To become overweight is usually caused by taking in more calories through eating without being able to shed these off through physical activity or exercise. Some of the known factors that contribute to this condition are:

- Eating disorders like binge eating
- Alcoholism
- Genetic Predisposition
- Hormonal imbalance like hypothyroidism
- Sedentary lifestyle or poor eating choices
- Poor nutrition
- Metabolic disorders
- Rapid weight loss and weight gain from fad dieting
- Psychotropic medication such as olanzapine
- Smoking cessation and stimulant withdrawal
- Constant stress

Patients that are insulin dependent and those that overdose on insulin also gain a lot of weight while on the other hand those that are already overweight develop insulin resistance and develop type 2 diabetes.

## Diagnosis

Persons that are overweight should check if they have the ff:

### Waist Measurement

One of the best ways to know if you are at a healthy weight is through your waist size. Typically, a waist circumference that is greater than 35 inches in women and 40 inches in men is a good predictor that the person may be overweight. Waist data above these numbers indicates a high degree of stomach fat, a dangerous type of fat that encircles vital organs and increases the risk of also getting type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure and cholesterol and the metabolic syndrome. The advantage of using this tool is it is quick to perform as all you really need is a measuring tape. Do this by placing the tape on top of the hip bone and bring it around your body and then level it with the belly button. Try to resist the temptation of sucking your stomach fat in as taking an inaccurate reading is only going to make it harder for you to know if you are at a healthy weight level or not.

### Excessive Snoring

If someone you know such as a friend or partner is excessively snoring and wakes up restless in the night then it might be a good idea to check the weight. Sleep apnea is a condition that causes your breathing to stop and begin again while asleep, this can cause loss of precious oxygen and results in severe fatigue during the waking hours. When your overweight, fat accumulates around the neck region and this can narrow the airway resulting in shallow breathing or possibly pauses in breathing.

### Frequent Heartburn

Body weight changes even small ones can result in more acid reflux. More than a third of overweight people show symptoms of GERD or gastroesophageal reflux and data from research shows evidence of a connection between the two. Other symptoms you might encounter are belching, nausea and pain in the stomach and a bitter taste in the mouth.

### Pain in the joints

The most common kind of arthritis is osteoarthritis and this is a disabling disorder that causes progressive deterioration of joints and decreased mobility. Carrying around too much weight on your body put too much pressure on your joints. If you notice that you have stiff or achy hips and joints, chronic back pain that is not associated with any other medical condition apart from weight then you might be overweight.

### Health Risks of Being Overweight

Trouble Breathing – A larger body mass may prevent your lungs from completely expanding and your breathing muscles from completely working as they should, the result is you cannot take enough air in. Inflammation associated to being overweight can affect your lungs as you can get tired pretty easily while doing normal daily activities such as climbing a flight of stairs. Also the additional weight can make people with asthma and chronic obstructive lung disease have worse symptoms.

### Fatty Liver

Cholesterol is manufactured in the liver and when you eat too much fattening and processed foods the excess fat eventually builds up in the liver and produce scar tissue known as cirrhosis, which can cause liver failure. 20% of patients with non-alcohol fatty liver disease progress to liver cirrhosis. Symptoms may not show until irreversible damage has taken place.

### High Levels of Bad Cholesterol

LDL or low density lipoprotein also known as bad cholesterol is responsible for blocking the arteries causing serious heart problems. Eating unhealthy foods will cause you to become overweight and raise LDL cholesterol as well as triglyceride levels. Foods containing soluble fiber such as oats and other whole grains, beans, okra, apples, strawberries and eggplant can lower your cholesterol levels.

### Increased risk of Type 2 Diabetes

There is no doubt that belly fat is connected to insulin resistance. This is the condition wherein your body makes enough insulin but your cells are unable to utilize them properly to transport glucose from the blood. The result is higher levels of blood sugar in the blood and diabetes.
