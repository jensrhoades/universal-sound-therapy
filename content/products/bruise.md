## Short Description of Bruising

Bruising is one of the most common skin injuries around. It results in skin discoloration due to injured blood vessels that lie beneath and begins to collect near the surface. This results in the ubiquitous black and blue mark seen in the patient.

## Symptoms of Bruising

- Initially a bruise may be reddish in color then it will turn blue or dark purple in just a few hours and then yellow or green after a few days as it already heals
- It is usually tender and painful for the first few days but the pain goes away when healing happens as the color fades
- There is no risk of infection as there is no break in the skin

## About Bruising

A bruise or contusion appears when the skin is exposed to external trauma such as a cut or blow to any area of the body. This results in the tiny blood vessels called capillaries to burst. The blood gets trapped below the skin's surface which results in a bruise.

## Etiology of Bruises

Anyone can get a bruise when you bump into something or something bumps into you. Bruises can occur in some patients who exercise a lot like weight lifters and athletes. The bruising comes from the microscopic tears in the blood vessel under the skin.

Bruises in the elderly occur more often due to the skin having thinned due to age. Bruises can also occur if a patient is taking certain drugs such as blood thinners.

## Diagnosis of Bruises

If the cause has been immediately ascertained such as an injury and the healthcare provider does not suspect any broken bones, then no other tests may be ordered. However, if there is severe pain and swelling on the area, the doctor may ask for an x-ray exam.

## Conditions that Cause Bruising

### Sports Injuries

Occur as a result of trauma from exercising or while participating in a sporting activity. They include broken bones, strains, sprains, dislocations, ripped tendons and swelling of the muscles.

### Concussion

A mild traumatic brain injury that results after an impact to the head or after a whiplash injury.

### Thrombocytopenia

A medical condition that results in a low platelet count. Symptoms include: red, purple or brown bruises, nosebleeds, gum bleeding, prolonged bleeding.

### Leukemia

Multiple kinds of blood cancer that happen when the white blood cells in the marrow grow out of control.

### Von Willebrand Disease

A kind of bleeding disorder that occurs due to a deficiency of von Willebrand clotting factor. Most common symptoms are easy bruising, excessive nose bleeding, prolonged bleeding after an injury.

### Head Injuries

Considered to be a medical emergency. Refers to any injury that involves the skull, scalp or brain.

### Ankle Sprain

Bruising can occur as a result of an ankle sprain — an injury that involves the ligaments that surround and connect the bones of the leg and foot.
