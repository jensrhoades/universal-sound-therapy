## About Personal Growth

To achieve personal growth requires motivation, the desire to improve one’s self, and the willingness to strive to make the changes. One also has to get out of their comfort zone and at times do things that are uncomfortable but for one’s own good.An open mind is also necessary and the desire to learn and grow is also important.Personal growth is also an ongoing process that begins from an early age but is mostly shaped by parents, teachers, and the environment.However, in order to make the most out of it, you also need to become aware of the process, know what it is and also take the necessary steps to grow and improve.There are times when problems and difficulties are the triggers that awaken the desire to grow and lead to making changes in one’s life.There are also times when people are inspired to take steps toward personal growth after they read an inspiring book, watch a movie or read about people who achieved success.

More often than not it leads people to new paths, to making changes in their lives, expanding their knowledge and self-awareness, improving skills, and developing new ones.Personal growth can assist you in all areas of your life and it can also help you at work.It can also change your attitude toward work, and therefore, open new avenues for advancement.Personal growth can help in growing emotionally and mentally as well as becoming a more considerate, loving and positive person.It can also help you see your mistakes, and negative habits and allow you to learn the steps in correcting and changing them.

## Importance of Personal Growth

Aside from the intrinsic value of learning new things and advancing yourself as a person, the very act of pursuing personal growth distinguishes people from others.When you choose to act on personal growth, you instantly reflect a desire for self-improvement and growth.You exhibit personal qualities that are highly valued for success in your career, whether it works for a company or for a personal business. It does not matter what level you are at present or where your skillsets or qualities are at the moment, the need for improvement attitude is the factor that will push through and bring your work from drab to fab.The first and most important step is to actively want to pursue your personal growth.The next steps may be a little vague as a lot of people inevitably feel a sense of restlessness and wander while on their journey.However, the important thing is to not forget that this difficult journey is in itself a kind of personal growth.Therefore it is important to cherish each moment of feeling puzzled and lost.

## Goals of Personal Growth

- Embracing Empathy — This is about objectively understanding varying perspectives which in turn provides a wealth of insight into your own perspective.
- Instilling Confidence – There are many studies that show us that a person’s IQ is not the most important component for success.Instead, self-confidence, goal setting and perseverance are considered far more essential than just intelligence itself.A person’s self-esteem or self-confidence is what he or she thinks about himself or herself. A very good reason for improving self-confidence is the proven strong association between it and success.Personal growth therefore requires achieving a healthy level of self-confidence.
- Actively Listening – By actively learning to pay attention and demonstrate to others that you truly value their opinions and what they have to say is a sign of good personal growth. You can do this by actively listening to the other person, providing an open-ended question and removing any distractions that can hinder your ability to listen.
- Facing Fears – In order to get rid of fear one must be exposed to it in order for you to let yourself feel afraid.Once you have achieved a comfortable sense of mind with the ambiguity and uncertainty of the situation, you can start working your way through in a calm and rational manner.
- Getting Along with Others – It is important to always find ways to create rapport with others.It is also important that you are honest and your primary objective should not be to manipulate others, but learn ways through which you can relate and get along with them.
