## About Candida

There are many kinds of fungi that grow normally in the human body. One of the most common is called Candida. This is a kind of yeast that lives normally in places like the mouth and belly as well as the skin without causing any issues. However, in certain conditions, the yeast can overgrow out of control resulting to disease.

## Etiology of Candida Disease

Normally, the fungus is kept under control by the healthy bacteria that lives in the body. If the bacteria presence is compromised or if the immune system is weakened due to disease or certain drugs, Candida can overproduce.

Factors that can result in Candida contagion:
- Prolonged antibiotic use
- A high diet in sugar and refined carbs
- High alcohol intake
- An immunocompromised body
- Use of oral contraceptives
- Presence of diabetes mellitus
- High stress levels

## Kinds of Candida Infection

### Recurring Genital or Urinary Tract Infections

It is approximated that over seventy five percent of all women will get at least one vaginal yeast overgrowth in their lifetime.

### Digestive Problems

The overall health of the digestive tract relies mostly on the balance between good and bad bacteria. Candida overgrowth is associated with several gastrointestinal tract problems such as ulcerative colitis and Crohn's disease.

### Skin and Nail Fungal Problems

Overgrowth of Candida may cause conditions like athlete's foot, ringworm and toenail fungal issues.
