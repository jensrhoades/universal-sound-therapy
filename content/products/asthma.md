## Short Description of Asthma

Asthma is a medical condition wherein the airways become narrow and swell to produce additional mucus. The result can make breathing difficult and induce coughing fits, a whistling sound or wheezing when you breathe out as well as shortness of breath.

### Symptoms of Asthma

- Shortness of breath
- Chest tightness or pain
- Wheezing when exhaling
- Trouble sleeping caused by shortness of breath, coughing or wheezing
- Coughing or wheezing attacks that are worsened by a respiratory virus

## About Asthma

Asthma is a chronic disease of the airways and lungs. The function of the airways or bronchial tubes is to let air in and out of the lungs. Patients that have asthma have airways that are always inflamed. The airway become more swollen and the surrounding respiratory lung muscles begin to tighten when something triggers the symptoms.

### Etiology of Asthma

There is no real cause as to why some patients develop asthma and others don't. However, many researchers believe that it is a blend of environmental and inherited factors.

### Triggers of Asthma

- Stress and strong emotions
- Cold air inhaled to the lungs
- Strenuous physical activity or cigarette smoke
- Respiratory infections like the common cold
- Inhalation of airborne allergens such as pollen, dust mites, mold, pet dander
- Sulfites and preservatives added to certain food and beverages
- Drugs like aspirin, beta-blockers, NSAIDs
- Presence of GERD

### Risk Factors for Developing Asthma

- First degree relative that is a known asthmatic
- Presence of another allergic condition
- Overweight or obese
- Cigarette smoker
- Constant exposure to second hand cigarette smoke
- Constant exposure to exhaust fumes and other kinds of pollution

## Types of Asthma

### Exercise Induced Asthma

Also known as EIB or exercise-induced broncho-constriction. Symptoms of the disease develop when lung airways start to narrow due to exertion or physical activity.

### Allergic Asthma

Sufferers produce signs and symptoms when exposed to certain allergens like pollen, dust and pet dander.

### Adult-Onset Asthma

Some people do not exhibit symptoms of this airway health condition until they reach adulthood.

### Occupational Asthma

When you begin experiencing symptoms at work — typically from chemical fumes, dust and other air irritants.

### Non-Allergic Asthma

A disease triggered by illness, certain drugs and environmental factors.
