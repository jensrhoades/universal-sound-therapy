## What is Sound Therapy?

Sound therapy, and music therapy, is a form of alternative medicine that uses sound as a healing modality. Sound therapy has been found to be an effective tool, in more recent years, for stimulating the brain. Proponents of sound therapy argue that it is particularly effective in stimulating the brain due to its multisensory approach. For example, binaural beats involve two different tones on top of one another and are believed to affect the listener's mental state by promoting entrainment — synchronization between two or more psychological centers or rhythms in the brain. Meanwhile, monaural beats consists of a single frequency; however, by activating contralateral neurons in both hemispheres of the brain simultaneously, it can still help stimulate mental activity.

Critics argue that sound therapy is not necessarily effective or helpful when it comes to stimulating the brain. Similarly, while monaural beats involve a single frequency pattern, no evidence exists suggesting that this type of auditory stimulus is more powerful than any other type of sound stimulus.

With regards to stimulating the brain through sound therapy, results are mixed and much research is yet to be done to investigate this further. Nevertheless, research offers some evidence that neural plasticity may come about as part of listening to certain types of music or auditory stimuli.
