## Symptoms and Effects of ADHD

Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD) is a neurodevelopmental disorder that affects 5-7% of the population. The effects of ADHD are highly variable and vary from person to person; individuals with ADHD may experience physical health problems such as insomnia, chronic lateness and procrastination, anxiety or depression resulting from social isolation, trouble understanding complex emotions due to the lack of emotional control, academic underperformance due to inattentiveness or lack of effort and focus on a task.
