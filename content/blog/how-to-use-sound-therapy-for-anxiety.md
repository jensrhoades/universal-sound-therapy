## What is Sound Therapy?

Sound therapy is a type of non-invasive therapy that uses audible or subliminal sound waves or frequencies  to promote emotional health, psychological growth, and physical healing. Sound therapy may be used to reduce stress and anxiety by creating a calming environment or to help with managing sleep disorders by introducing low, calming tones. It can also be used therapeutically in the treatment of many mental and physical conditions, including depression, bipolar disorder, PTSD, and substance abuse. In recent years, sound therapy has become popularly known for its effectiveness in alleviating anxiety.

The efficacy of sound therapy has been widely debated. Among skeptics, the idea of using sound waves to help alleviate mental anguish is viewed as dubious at best. Meanwhile, proponents emphasize the robust research that exists supporting the positive effects of sound waves on physical and psychological wellbeing. Although evidence-based studies quantifying the effects of sound therapy remain scanty due to difficulty in measuring its direct impacts on anxiety symptoms, increasing numbers of people are turning to this form of therapeutic intervention daily in search of relief from psychological distress.

## Commonly Asked Questions

### What type of sound therapy is best for reducing anxiety?

The type of sound therapy that is best for reducing anxiety largely depends on the individual's needs, but evidence suggests that music-based sound therapy can be particularly effective as can cymatic therapy. Research indicates that listening to certain types of music can reduce stress and anxiety, as well as help regulate mood, improve cognitive performance, and decrease heart rate and blood pressure. Specifically, slower tempo instrumental melodies with consistent low frequencies have been found to be the most beneficial in terms of relaxation. The therapist-guided practice of healing sound meditation, which involves specific chants and mantras, has also been known to help reduce stress and anxiety. In any case, the best approach to sound therapy for reducing anxiety would ultimately depend on the individual's needs and preferences.

### How quickly can sound therapy help reduce anxiety?

Sound therapy can help reduce feelings of anxiety quickly, often without any medication or treatment. Studies have shown that listening to soothing music for just 15 minutes a day can reduce anxiety levels significantly within a few weeks. Listening to calming music can help slow down heart rate and breathing, decrease cortisol levels, and create a relaxed state of mind. Additionally, it can activate the body's natural relaxation response which in turn can help alleviate anxious thoughts and emotion. Therefore, sound therapy can be an effective tool to quickly reduce symptoms of anxiety.

### Is sound therapy a long-term solution for anxiety?

No, sound therapy is not a long-term solution for anxiety. While it may be beneficial in the short-term, such as providing respite from acute anxiety symptoms and promoting relaxation, its efficacy diminishes over time. It's important to seek out counseling or psychotherapy if you are struggling with prolonged anxiety. Sound therapy can help bolster these existing treatments for long-term success. In addition, lifestyle changes such as proper exercise, healthy nutrition, and stress management techniques can also provide lasting results for managing anxiety.
