# Sound Therapy at Home: How to Use it to Manage ADHD Symptoms

ADHD symptoms can be frustrating and debilitating, but there are many ways to treat the condition. One increasingly popular form of therapy is sound therapy, a type of mind-body healing that is effective, non-invasive, and can easily be practiced from the comfort of your own home.

## What is Sound Therapy for ADHD?

Sound therapy for ADHD, also known as sound sensory treatment or sound integration therapy, is a non-conventional intervention specifically designed to support the neurological development of individuals with ADHD. It involves using music, sounds and voice tones to reduce distractibility and increase focus.

## The Benefits of Sound Therapy

Sound therapy has been long known as a means of reducing stress, inducing relaxation and reducing symptoms of ADHD. The benefits range from improved mood and increased focus, to better sleep quality and reduced impulsivity. Research has shown that sound therapy may have positive effects on both brain chemistry and circadian rhythms, leading to improved attention span, less irritability, better sleep patterns, improved impulse control and better academic performance.

## How Can You Use Sound Therapy at Home?

- **White noise**: Play white noise in the background while studying to block out distracting external sounds
- **Calming music**: Listen to music with tempo-consistency arrangements (TCA) to help achieve a relaxing mental state
- **Soundscapes**: Natural sounds like rain or birds chirping can create a calming environment
- **Mindful listening**: Pay close attention to sounds and become fully immersed in them

## How Can Sound Therapy Help Children with ADHD?

Research has shown that sound therapy can have a positive impact on activity levels and improve attention. One study found that when participants listened to 30-minute sessions of binaural beats five days a week for four weeks, their reported symptoms—ranging from mood disturbances, impulsivity, hyperactivity, and inattention—all significantly decreased.

## Conclusion

Sound therapy has been found to be an effective and safe way to manage and reduce the symptoms associated with ADHD in both adults and children. The key factor in its success is tailoring the treatment plan according to individual needs and preferences.
