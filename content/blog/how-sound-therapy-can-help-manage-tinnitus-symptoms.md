# How Sound Therapy Can Help Manage Tinnitus Symptoms

Are the ringing in your ears driving you crazy? Tinnitus, or the perception of sound that has no external source, affects countless people around the world, leading to a significant decrease in quality of life. Fortunately, sound therapy is a promising treatment option.

## What is Sound Therapy for Tinnitus?

Sound therapy is based on the idea that exposing individuals with tinnitus to soothing, calming sounds or specific predetermined frequencies will reduce their tinnitus symptoms and help them manage related distress.

## Types of Sound Therapy

The most common approaches include: cymatic sound therapy, auditory modifications, white noise machines, tinnitus retraining therapy (TRT), hypnosis, cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), and deep brain stimulation (DBS).

### Cymatic Sound Therapy

Cymatic Sound Therapy is based on the work of Dr. Peter Guy Manners, a British Osteopath who started working with sound as a healing tool in the 1960s. His work showed that a person's natural healthy frequencies can be altered by illness or injury, and that reintroducing the correct frequencies back to the body allows the body to correct itself and return to a healthy state.

## Masking

Masking involves using external sound sources to distract from or mask the presence of tinnitus, helping reduce temporary or chronic tinnitus symptoms.

## Habituation

Habituation is a process of reducing the emotional reactivity to the tinnitus symptom. By engaging in habituation, tinnitus sufferers can gradually adapt to their condition and eventually diminish their emotional reaction to the sound.

## How to Use Sound Therapy to Manage Tinnitus

There are three main methods: Cymatics, masking, and retraining. Cymatics uses replacing damaged frequencies with new healthy ones. Masking uses a noise louder than the tinnitus to cover it up. Retraining works on reconditioning the brain to lessen the impact of tinnitus.

## Conclusion

Sound therapy can be an effective form of management and treatment for tinnitus symptoms. For those with mild to moderate tinnitus, it can bring noise levels down to a comfortable level while improving quality of life.
